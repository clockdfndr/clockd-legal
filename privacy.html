Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Clockd app collects, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. Clockd is run by the Clockd team in Australia ("we", "us"). We aim to handle your information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

The short version
  • We collect what Clockd needs to work: your account details, the photos you take in the app, and your training activity (clock-ins, streaks, XP, battles).
  • We don't sell your information and we don't show ads.
  • Transformation battle photos are sent to an AI service (Anthropic) only to judge that battle. Anthropic doesn't use them to train its AI.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time (Settings → Account → Delete account).

1. Who can use Clockd

Clockd is for people aged 16 and over. Some features (the 3-week mini cut transformation battle) are for people aged 18 and over. If you're under 16, please don't use Clockd. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 16, we'll delete it.

Camera and photos. Clockd uses your camera to take clock-in, battle and transformation photos. These must be taken in the app (this keeps battles fair). Clockd only uses your photo library to choose a profile picture, and only the picture you pick is uploaded.

We don't collect your location, contacts, health data or advertising ID, and we don't use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

3. Who can see what

  • Public profile: anyone on Clockd can see your profile, pinned photos and clock-ins.
  • Private profile: only people whose follow request you accept can see your clock-ins and pinned photos. Your name, @handle and profile picture are still visible so people can find you.
  • Battles: check-ins, chat and transformation photos are only visible to you and your opponent.
  • Verse Yourself: your weights and progress photos are visible to you only.
  • Leaderboards: your name, profile picture, level and streak can appear on leaderboards.
  • Blocking hides you and the other person from each other.

4. Services that help run Clockd

We use a few trusted companies to run the app. They only get the information they need for their job.

This means some of your information is stored or processed outside Australia. We choose providers that protect data with security measures such as encryption.

About the AI judge. When a transformation battle ends, both people's photos and goals are sent to Anthropic's AI so it can pick a winner. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, it doesn't use this data to train its AI models and automatically deletes it from its systems, normally within 30 days. The AI only credits visible progress. It doesn't give medical advice and its verdict isn't a health assessment.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't use your information for advertising.
  • We don't share your information with anyone else unless the law requires us to.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open.

When you delete your account:

  • Your account is hidden from everyone straight away and you're signed out.
  • For 30 days, you can change your mind by signing back in, and everything is restored.
  • After 30 days, we permanently delete your account, photos (including Verse Yourself photos and weights), profile, clock-ins, XP, streaks and messages.
  • Live battles are forfeited when you delete. Finished battles stay in your opponents' history, with your side shown as "Deleted user" (no name, picture or photos).
  • Copies in our providers' routine backups can take a short extra time to clear out.

7. Security

Your data is encrypted in transit and stored with access controls, so people can only see what they're allowed to. Battle and transformation photos are kept in private storage. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we'll tell you as required by law.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile at any time.
  • Make your profile private in Settings → Privacy.
  • Turn notifications off by type in Settings → Push notifications, or in your iPhone settings.
  • Block and report people and posts.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct it, by emailing us.

9. Contact and complaints

Questions or privacy concerns? Email support email coming soon. We'll reply within 30 days.

If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is important, we'll also tell you in the app.
